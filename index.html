The 2-Minute Rule
If a task takes less than two minutes to complete, do it immediately. This prevents small tasks from piling up and causing mental clutter.

Spaced Repetition
You learn better when you review information at gradually increasing intervals. It forces your brain to work harder to recall it, making the memory stick forever.
